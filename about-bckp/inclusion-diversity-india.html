A commitment to our people

We are constantly taking action to create more employment and advancement opportunities for people from all backgrounds. Not just make our workplace welcoming for all, we strive to ensure that all our people—no matter their gender identity, sexual orientation or disabilities—receive equal pay for equal work throughout their career journey. Here’s how we are making a real difference:

  1. GETTING TO 50/50
  2. UNLEASHING ABILITY
  3. SHOWING PRIDE
  4. BUILDING RESILIENCE

Our goal is to create a 50/50 gender-balanced workforce by 2025, and enabled by Vaahini—our networking forum for women professionals—we are making steady progress. The platform is helping us create holistic experiences that empower women to grow, personally and professionally. Through programs such as High-Tech Women and Grow Women in Leadership, we are nurturing future women leaders and leveling the playing field for them. Moreover, initiatives like Returning Mothers program and Career Reboot are providing crucial support and tools women need to seamlessly reintegrate back into the workforce after a break.

READ MORE

We are enabling change for our people with visible and invisible disabilities to perform at their full potential. How? By thinking accessible—whether it’s deploying assistive technologies or providing reasonable accommodations with workplace adjustments and transport arrangements. And with accessibility tools that allows all our people to contribute equally. Currently, 75% of our applications are accessible. What’s more, at the India Accessibility Center of Excellence, we are constantly working on making technology more inclusive. With programs like Abilities Unleashed, we are helping our persons with disabilities explore career opportunities they may not have considered before and become authentic leaders and role models.

READ MORE

Empowering all our people to live authentically and putting their well-being first, irrespective of gender identity or sexual orientation, are top priorities for us. Wherever possible, we provide identical employee benefits to same-sex and opposite-sex partners. We are leading change in the country with policies and benefits focused on the caregiver rather than marital status or gender. Partners of our LGBTIQ+ people enjoy insurance benefits; transmen who can bear children can avail the 26-week maternity leave; and all secondary caregivers no matter their gender are eligible for paternal, surrogacy and adoption leave. We are also breaking barriers with programs like the Accenture Inclusive Internship, which is all about hands-on experience and relevant work experience to build future careers for young transgenders and other marginalized youth.

READ MORE

Our support for mental health and disability inclusion accelerates our culture of equality and helps everyone to thrive. With our Let's Talk program, we are breaking down the stigma around mental health by encouraging open, honest conversations—with family, friends, colleagues or our Employee Assistance Program (EAP) experts—and building mental resilience for our people. EAP offers 24/7 free and confidential services which is extended to the families of our people as well. It helps our people with a variety of issues such as managing stress at home or workplace, legal and financial issues and relationship problems.

Vaahini: Nurturing women professionals

We are promoting gender equality in India by helping women professionals build connections and develop support systems to thrive at the workplace with Vaahini, a networking forum enabled by Accenture. This is a pioneering network working towards women empowerment in India and women in leadership. The forum offers talented women professionals in India—like YOU—an unparalleled opportunity to learn, share and empower themselves and others.

Get inspired by real-life stories, join pulsating discussions, exchange path-breaking ideas, explore career options, connect with Accenture leadership and more.
