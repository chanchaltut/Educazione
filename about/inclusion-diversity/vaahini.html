Reach your full potential

Join Vaahini, our community of women from across industries who are ushering in powerful change at work and outside. Since 2010, we’ve been championing women’s inclusion in the workforce and celebrating their achievements through inspiring content. 

Our series of Vaahini events, leadership podcasts, blogs and video interviews with female visionaries and leaders are designed to uplift and inspire. We’re empowering women with learnings and industry knowledge so they can be more creative, dream bigger, accelerate their careers and above all, be themselves.

Why Vaahini? Because gender equality is a top priority for Accenture. And through this platform, we’re building on our progress toward achieving a gender-balanced workforce by 2025. Today, women represent 47 percent of our workforce and 26 percent of our managing directors.
