Digital engineering and manufacturing careers

Using the combined power of data and digital, you’ll work together with our clients to reimagine the products they make and how they make them.

SEARCH INDUSTRY X JOBS

Digitize what you make.
Revolutionize how you make it.

Welcome to Industry X—our integrated approach to digital engineering and manufacturing.

Using data and technologies such as AR/VR/XR, cloud, AI, 5G, robotics and digital twins, you’ll work with our clients to design, engineer and manufacture products and services in ways that are more connected, more efficient and sustainable by design.

Join us to shape the future of industry, including automotive, aerospace and defense, consumer goods and services, energy, industrial, resources and life sciences. Accelerate ideas in The Dock, Innovation Network Centers and Accenture Labs. And drive impactful change with the power of deep ecosystem partnerships: AWS, Dassault Systèmes, Google, Microsoft, PTC, SAP and Siemens.

Our purpose is to deliver on the promise of technology and human ingenuity and our commitment to our people, clients and communities is to accelerate equality for all.

Take what you dream and make it real with Industry X.
