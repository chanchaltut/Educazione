Accenture Life Trends 2023

We recruit curious people with ideas and thoughts we want to hear and amplify, and our annual trends report (formerly known as Fjord Trends) channels the voices of our experts from around the world. Building on the report’s 15-year legacy, Accenture Life Trends 2023 identifies five global movements in human behavior that will influence business, culture and society in the coming year.

  • Accenture Song’s global network of designers, creatives, technologists, sociologists and anthropologists contributed their insights and intelligence to this year’s trends. If you want to be a key voice for next year’s report, join us at Accenture Song.
  • Accenture Life Trends 2023 looks at the shifts in control and power that are affecting how people, brands and organizations interact. Understanding these changes is central to business and marketing strategy in the coming twelve months and beyond.
