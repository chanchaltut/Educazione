Driving growth through people and technology

Change is constant and accelerating faster than ever. Transformation is compressed. Customer and employee expectations have radically shifted how companies must work and deliver. The C-Suite and Boards are being held accountable in ways we have not seen in the past.

To address today’s most pressing challenges and unlock the most compelling opportunities, leaders need to move fast.

While many believe innovative technology is the answer to rapid change, it's only part of the solution. The most successful companies elevate people and put them at the center of change.

Because nothing changes if people don’t.

We help leaders create transformation by enriching the relationship between people and technology.

We help clients to reimagine their business, build workforces with the leaders and cultures to accelerate change, and create and deliver employee experience and HR services. Our focus on sustainability, inclusion, diversity & equity (ID&E), purpose, and board effectiveness helps CEOs drive a Workforce Transformation agenda.

Talent transformation in a world of uncertainty

Organizations are navigating the energy crisis, supply chain stress, inflation, a tight talent market and a widening skills gap. Workforce transformation is a strategic evolution of skills, capabilities and organizational hardwiring to unlock growth amid disruption.

  1. THE KEY TO UNLOCKING VALUE AND EMPLOYEE POTENTIAL
  2. A NEW STYLE OF LEADERSHIP FOR A NEW ERA
  3. HOW MODERN CEOS CAN CLOSE THE VALUE GAP

Disruption, the well-being revolution, a new generation of workers... global change has inspired people to rethink their relationships with work. But leaders generally don't see the impact of these changes on culture, overestimating the connectedness of their people by 2X.

Learn how to unlock two-thirds of a person's potential at work and reach a 7.5% revenue growth premium per year through omni-connected experiences. By driving more vibrant relationships, these experiences increase the sense of personal and measurable business impact to help people thrive.

Transform how you work

You've heard about "quiet quitting" and the Great Resignation, but employees aren't the only ones going through a period of change. Leaders also have an opportunity to shed the 20th century "command and control" model in favor of a leadership style that's agile, responsible and connected, driving tangible results as they go.

Modernizing your approach is especially important in times of uncertainty and compressed transformation. Take these six actions to break down siloes, empower employees and foster a culture that drives innovation, productivity and retention.

Create a winning culture

Today, businesses and public service organizations are expected to operate with purpose, delivering financial results alongside social and environmental impact. Yet, Accenture research says 43% of the world's largest organizations lack the ability to deliver multidimensional value as intended.

Closing the gap starts with embracing pandemic lessons, rethinking how a successful CEO acts and embedding sustainability into the organization. Companies infused with "Sustainability DNA" outperform peers by 21% on profitability as well as positive environmental and societal outcomes—which means every stakeholder wins

Advancing inclusion, diversity & equity

Customer, workforce and shareholder expectations have vaulted Inclusion, Diversity & Equity (ID&E) as a major priority for C-suite leaders.

Businesses are under great pressure to deliver value for all shareholders: to turn their responsible goals into actions and create lasting impact. Those that succeed are sustainable organizations which embrace ID&E at their core.

ID&E is a powerful driver of growth, brand, and societal impact, creating value for workforce, customers, shareholders and communities. From opening up new pathways for attracting, engaging and retaining talent, to creating diverse teams that foster innovation and new market opportunities, companies that embed ID&E across their organization amplify their purpose and values while furthering growth objectives.

"C-suite leaders are driving sustainable business practices, which include ensuring a workforce that’s inclusive and diverse in every way," said Christie Smith, lead for Talent & Organization at Accenture.
